Hi! I’m Kabilan Mahathevan, a PhD student at [Virginia Tech](https://www.vt.edu/)'s [Computer Science department](https://cs.vt.edu/). I am fortunate enough to be advised by [Prof. Kirshanthan ("Krish") Sundararajah](https://kirshanthans.github.io/). My research focuses on building tools and techniques that make tensor computations efficient, reliable, and practical for real-world computational workloads.

I'm currently focused on sparse tensor computation, where most entries of a tensor are zero, as is common in scientific simulations, graph analytics, and machine learning workloads. Because so much of the data can be skipped, sparse tensors are stored in compressed formats and processed with irregular, data-dependent control flow, which makes them notoriously difficult for compilers and hardware to optimize. My work explores how to make the compilers targeting these workloads faster and more trustworthy.

If you’re interested in this area or would like to discuss related research, feel free to reach out.

Outside of research, I watch an unhealthy amount of anime.
